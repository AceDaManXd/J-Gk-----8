# [[GK]] Sailor Denim Jacket NO.1

## Overview
An asymmetrical raw denim jacket inspired by military sailor silhouettes with oversized diagonal zip and sterling silver hardware.

## Design Intent
Raw and military, with silver that ages. The tarnish is a feature, and each jacket ships with a silver cleaning kit.
