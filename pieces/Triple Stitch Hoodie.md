# [[GK]] Triple Stitch Hoodie

## Overview

A heavyweight boxy hoodie built around visible 3-needle coverstitching, with a hidden pocket and rotating colorways that carry printed cars.

## Design Intent

The stitch is the constant; the car is the variable. Three parallel lines of stitching are the signature on every colorway (lane markings / racing stripes), and each colorway swaps in a different printed car.

> Rework: the removable hood, heavy distressing, and "loud counterpart to the Heavy Knit" framing are dropped. The Heavy Knit comparison is no longer needed to define this piece.
