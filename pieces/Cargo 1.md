# [[GK]] [[Cargo 1]]

## Overview
A 2-part modular cargo pant with a detachable front accent panel, contrast fabrics, and functional hanging canvas hardware.

## Design Intent
Workwear that comes apart. The panel swap and the hidden paisley lining are the surprise; the embossed leather tag is the signature.

## Construction
* 2-part main pant system with detachable front panel
* Panel attaches via corner buttons and zipper accents
* Separate hanging canvas strap from below front belt loop to front accent panel

---

## Hardware & Trims
* Vintage-style brass zippers (main + panel attachments)
* Dark polished wooden buttons
* Brass D-ring buckle on canvas belt

---

## Branding & Placement
* GK globe embroidery, upper-right back thigh
* Embossed floral / western-tooled leather patch, outer rear waistband, cream/gold border stitch
* Small leather brand tag inside rear waistband
