## Overview

A coated technical shell with a normal hood and a pair of loops sewn down at the bottom hem. Each loop runs in a U shape from the front shoulder area to the back, anchored to the hem at the same point on both sides, with slack that lets it flap a little.

## Design Intent

The loop is the concept: a decorative silhouette feature that is part of the garment, not a removable accessory. Bright colors make the loop read against the body. Contrast is borrowed from existing outdoor-brand jackets but kept low: the loop straps are always black with a slightly lighter glossy printedseatbelt pattern, and each colorway gets the classic box logo in a single color, raised in rugged rubber.

## Construction

- Normal hood
- Loops attach at the shoulder / front, run in a U to the back, and are stuck down on the bottom hem at the same spot front and back
- Loops read as seatbelt-style straps: black base with a slightly lighter glossy printed woven pattern (print, not woven-in)
- Loops are permanently attached (no snap or fastener) and may flap slightly
- No raw edges anywhere: bound, taped, or sealed finishes
- Articulated sleeves
