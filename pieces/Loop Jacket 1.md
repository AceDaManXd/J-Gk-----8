# [[GK]] Loop Jacket

## Overview
A technical shell with free-hanging side panels that loop off the body and fasten at the bottom.

## Design Intent
The loop is the concept: panels hang off the sides as a decorative silhouette, and a bottom fastener lets the wearer close them against the body.
