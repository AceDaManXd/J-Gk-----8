## Risks and tests (phase 4)

|Area|Item|Risk|Test / mitigation|Result|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Fabric|Shrinkage mismatch|White denim, green selvedge, corduroy, and paisley shrink differently; panel and body pucker after first wash|Pre-wash or pre-shrink every fabric; get per-fabric numbers from factory||
|Fabric|Color transfer|Dark or selvedge fabric bleeding onto white denim|Wet and dry rub test; research aging and wear||
|Fabric|Viscose lining|Slippery and weak at seams|Stable, tight stitch; reinforced pockets||
|Hardware|Brass zippers|Vintage brass can tarnish or transfer color onto white denim|Test, or use antique-finish brass; make sure it ages nicely||
|Hardware|Wooden buttons|Cracking in the wash; buttonholes on thick fabric|Wash test; size buttonholes carefully||
|Branding|Leather tag|Doesn't survive washing|Removable outer rear patch; wash-test inner tag||
|Fit|Detachable panel|Distorts the front fit|Check on fit model in phase 3||

## Risks and tests (phase 5)

|Area|Item|Risk|Test / mitigation|Result|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Construction|Sole bond|Heavy lugged outsole separates from upper|Flex and peel test; confirm construction method with factory||
|Leather|Distressed brown|Distressing is hard to repeat consistently across pairs|Approve a master swatch; agree tolerance with factory||
|Leather|Matte black|Scuffs or color rubs off|Rub test; confirm finish||
|Thread|Metallic silver|Wears or fades on high-abrasion seams|Abrasion test; confirm thread type||
|Hardware|Buckle|Weak point under repeated use or cast flaws|Pull and cycle test; check casting minimums||
|Hardware|Heel/toe plates|Loosen, scratch floors, or add weight|Wear test; confirm attachment method||
|Laces|Braided rope|Fraying at eyelets and speed hooks|Abrasion test; heat-seal or cap ends||
|Branding|Ankle logo|Embroidery or stamp wears at flex point|Flex test||
|Legal|Chicago flag imagery|Restrictions on using the flag design|Check before art is final (not legal advice; ask an IP attorney if unclear)||
