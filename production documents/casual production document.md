## Tasks by phase

### 0. Spec lock

- [ ] Define size range, grading, and measurements
- [ ] Decide how the diagonal tread crosses the placket and buttons (break the graphic or print over)
- [ ] Decide diagonal direction and how far it runs onto the sleeve
- [ ] Decide tread ink color for each colorway
- [ ] Decide hem text color (red hem text disappears on the red colorway)
- [ ] Decide side patch size and which side, and whether the globe patch is dropped
- [ ] Set target cost, target retail, MOQ, and sample rounds
- [ ] Pick the final name

### 1. Sourcing and quotes

- [ ] Source bamboo rib swatches in off-white, red, and sky blue
- [ ] Get lab dips for red and sky blue
- [ ] Source wooden four-hole buttons
- [ ] Source or make the embroidered tire patch
- [ ] Get knits cut-and-sew factory quotes and confirm MOQ
- [ ] Ask the factory for per-fabric shrinkage numbers

### 2. Proto sample

- [ ] Sample the first colorway only
- [ ] Build the tread art (dithered burnout, from a real tire photo)
- [ ] Make the wheel / tire patch artwork
- [ ] Prototype side patch attachment with a stabilizer on rib knit
- [ ] Check raw sleeve cuff and hem edges for curling and running
- [ ] Size buttonholes carefully for wooden buttons on rib knit

### 3. Fit sample

- [ ] Fit-test across the size range
- [ ] Check how the diagonal tread falls on the body and that it stays readable when worn
- [ ] Check the side patch doesn't pull or pucker the side seam

### 4. Wash/test

- [ ] Run the tests in the table below

### 5. Pre-production sample

- [ ] Confirm labels, packaging, and final trims
- [ ] Confirm all three colorways with approved lab dips and tread ink

## Risks and tests (phase 4)

|Area|Item|Risk|Test / mitigation|Result|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Graphic|DTF on stretch knit|Tread lines distort, crack, or peel when the rib knit stretches|Test DTF stretched, not flat; stretch and wash cycles||
|Graphic|Tread over placket|Print cracks or lifts over buttons and the placket seam|Test over and around the placket; consider breaking the art||
|Graphic|Ink contrast|Tread or hem text low-contrast on red or sky blue|Test ink colors on each fabric||
|Fabric|Bamboo rib shrinkage|Body and cuffs shrink differently; garment twists or shortens|Pre-shrink; get per-fabric numbers from factory||
|Fabric|Color transfer|Red bleeds onto the print or other garments in the wash|Wet and dry rub test; colorfastness test||
|Fabric|Raw edges|Cuffs and hem curl or run|Bound or overlocked edge that looks raw; wash test||
|Hardware|Wooden buttons|Crack or swell in the wash; buttonholes stretch out in rib knit|Wash test; size buttonholes carefully; reinforce||
|Branding|Side patch|Puckers the knit or pulls the side seam; embroidery wears in wash|Stabilizer behind patch; wash and pull test||
